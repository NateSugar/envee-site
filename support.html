Support

Questions, bugs, account help, or privacy requests: email closetapp.mail@gmail.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, closet, rankings, notes, photos, and comments. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

Report content or a member

Long-press any comment, or open a profile or item and choose Report. We review reports within 24 hours. You can also email us.

Copyright (DMCA) notices

Send notices to our designated agent at closetapp.mail@gmail.com. What to include is described in the Terms of Service, section 6.

Non-consensual intimate images

Email closetapp.mail@gmail.com with the subject line "Image removal". We remove valid requests within 48 hours.
